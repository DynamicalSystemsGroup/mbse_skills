# MBSE Literature Research

Research a bounded MBSE question and preserve what the evidence can support.

1. State the question, system/domain context, language version and intended use of the findings. Read [research evidence](references/research-evidence.md) for authority and publication distinctions.
2. Search source institutions, publishers, authors and references. Verify title, authors, DOI or versioned identifier, venue and publication status at an authoritative record.
3. Read full text before claiming what its method or results establish. Record the study context, assumptions, limitations, contrary findings and access gaps. A plausible title or search snippet is not bibliographic verification.
4. For code, confirm the paper links to the repository or the authors identify it. Record revision, license, tool requirements and whether reported results were independently reproduced.
5. Produce a concise synthesis separating direct source claims, supported interpretations and unknowns. Map each material conclusion to a source and explain relevance to this model or workflow.

A paper mentioning SysML may describe v1, v2, a profile or a proprietary extension. Determine which before transferring its examples. Institutional affiliation does not imply endorsement. Keep inaccessible or unidentified papers in the unresolved list; never invent quotes, citations, clauses or repository provenance.
