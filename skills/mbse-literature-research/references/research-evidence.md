# Research evidence

| Source | Appropriate use |
| --- | --- |
| OMG normative artifact | Language/API obligations in the specified revision. |
| INCOSE/NASA practice guidance | Methods and professional framing with edition and project applicability. |
| SEBoK | Orientation, vocabulary and references to follow to original sources. |
| Publisher or author paper | Specific method/results within study limits. |
| Implementation repository | Tool behavior, examples and reproducible implementation evidence. |

Record identifiers, publication status and the version actually read. Separate preprints from published versions and source claims from independent reproduction. Bibliographic existence is not proof that the method applies to SysML v2 or to the engineering domain at hand.

For a related repository, verify the author's link, revision, license, supported language/engine versions, example inputs and executable reproduction instructions. Report missing artifacts or irreproducible steps directly.

The INCOSE competency landing page and its public 2018 PDF can refer to different editions. Verify the document itself. Public descriptions of the paid handbook do not provide its detailed procedures. Access constraints should remain explicit.

A useful finding record contains: question, citation/identifier, version/status, claim, source passage or location, applicability, limitations, repository linkage, reproduction status, and unresolved issues. Link to sources and write original summaries; redistribution rights need their own inspection.
