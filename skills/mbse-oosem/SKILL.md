# MBSE OOSEM Workflow

Use this methodology extension when the user chooses OOSEM or asks for an OOSEM-oriented review. It is a starter adaptation of the public historical description, not the full tutorial or an implementation of OOSEM v2.

Read [source basis](references/source-basis.md) before making method-specific claims. Agree the edition and tailoring; preserve a requested process rather than silently switching methodologies.

Work iteratively at the requested system level:

1. Describe current limitations and desired stakeholder outcomes through enterprise/mission scenarios.
2. Establish black-box system requirements and their links to those scenarios.
3. Define logical responsibilities and interactions before choosing their realization.
4. Develop alternative allocations to hardware, software, data and procedures, preserving requirement traces.
5. Compare alternatives against explicit measures and constraints; record risks and decision rationale throughout the work.
6. Plan and assess verification and validation, linking observations back to requirements and stakeholder needs.

The activity sequence is a concise paraphrase of the historical survey. The following artifact contract is our original tailoring: retain stable IDs for needs, requirements, logical responsibilities, allocations, decisions and evidence; show their relationships in a table; mark each artifact proposed, observed or accepted with its authority. Iterate when a scenario, requirement or allocation changes, and list unresolved relationships.

Deliver a linked engineering argument and the explicitly limited evidence supporting it. Apply the core practices where available; a modeling language is chosen separately. A bounded energy calculation can exercise this workflow without establishing a complete architecture, mission validation or method compliance.
