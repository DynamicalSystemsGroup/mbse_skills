# OOSEM source basis and limits

Reviewed 2026-10-10.

- Jeff A. Estefan, *Survey of Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) Methodologies*, Rev. A, May 25, 2007, [official OMG-hosted PDF](https://www.omg.org/sysml/MBSE_Methodology_Survey_RevA.pdf), §3.2, printed pp. 16–20. This historical INCOSE focus-group survey describes a top-down approach, iterative and recursive activities, and SysML-based artifacts. It expressly provides a synopsis rather than a methodology assessment or the detailed tutorial. The six activity paraphrases in the skill come from §3.2.1, pp. 17–19.
- [INCOSE OOSEM working group](https://www.incose.org/group/object-oriented-se-method-working-group/) is an authoritative current entrypoint for the method's development. Its ongoing work does not establish that this historical adaptation implements newer OOSEM v2 guidance.

Our trace table, completion criteria and rover tailoring are original collection conventions. No full training material was accessed or reproduced, and no institutional endorsement is claimed. Before using edition-specific rules or claiming method compliance, obtain and review the relevant authoritative method material. Access restrictions remain visible.
