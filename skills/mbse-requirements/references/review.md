# Requirement review

These are original collection review conventions, not a certification checklist.

Record ID, statement, source/parent, rationale, operating conditions, verification method and observation, owner, status and open issues. A requirement with an unresolved threshold can be useful as a draft but is not ready for a pass/fail test. A design decision may constrain later requirements; record the decision and its authority rather than making the need appear to mandate it.

Read NASA's [technical requirements definition](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-0-system-design-processes/) §4.2 for institutional context and its [product realization](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/5-0-product-realization/) §§5.3–5.4 for verification and validation. NASA guidance has a NASA application context; project use requires tailoring. Consult the requested standard edition for exact obligations.
