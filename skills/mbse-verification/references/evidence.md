# Evidence scope

Original collection convention: record claim/requirement ID, criterion, method, exact artifact/configuration identity, inputs, observed result, raw evidence location, limitations and reviewer/decision status. Use explicit “not run” for an absent experiment and “undecided” for an inconclusive observation. Proposed acceptance is not stakeholder approval.

NASA [product verification and validation](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/5-0-product-realization/) §§5.3–5.4 supplies process context. Its scope is broader than checking a model in a language engine. Standards, organizational review and physical testing may impose additional evidence requirements.
